## 2. Part-A (50% Weightage) High-Yield Formulas & Rules Cheat Sheet

### 2.1 Quantitative Aptitude Essential Formulas
* **Percentages & Profit/Loss:**
  * Successive percentage changes of $a\%$ and $b\%$: $\text{Net Change} = \left(a + b + \frac{ab}{100}\right)\%$.
  * $\text{Profit \%} = \frac{\text{SP} - \text{CP}}{\text{CP}} \times 100$; $\text{Discount \%} = \frac{\text{MP} - \text{SP}}{\text{MP}} \times 100$; $\frac{\text{MP}}{\text{CP}} = \frac{100 + \text{Profit \%}}{100 - \text{Discount \%}}$.
* **Simple & Compound Interest:**
  * $\text{SI} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$; $\text{Amount (CI)} = P\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T$.
  * **2-Year CI vs. SI Difference:** $\text{CI}_2 - \text{SI}_2 = P\left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2$.
  * **3-Year CI vs. SI Difference:** $\text{CI}_3 - \text{SI}_3 = P\left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2 \left(3 + \frac{R}{100}\right)$.
* **Time, Speed & Distance:**
  * $\text{Average Speed (Equal Distances at } u \text{ and } v\text{)} = \frac{2uv}{u + v}$ (Harmonic Mean).
  * **Relative Speed:** Same direction $= |u - v|$; Opposite direction $= (u + v)$; Conversion: $1\text{ m/s} = \frac{18}{5}\text{ km/hr} = 3.6\text{ km/hr}$.
  * **Boats & Streams:** Downstream speed $D = B + S$; Upstream speed $U = B - S \implies \text{Boat Speed } B = \frac{D+U}{2}$, $\text{Stream Speed } S = \frac{D-U}{2}$.
* **Time & Work:**
  * $\frac{M_1 \times D_1 \times H_1}{W_1} = \frac{M_2 \times D_2 \times H_2}{W_2}$. If A takes $x$ days and B takes $y$ days, together they take $\frac{xy}{x+y}$ days.
* **Mixtures & Alligation:**
  * $\frac{\text{Quantity of Cheaper}}{\text{Quantity of Dearer}} = \frac{\text{Dearer Price} - \text{Mean Price}}{\text{Mean Price} - \text{Cheaper Price}}$.
  * After $n$ operations of removing $x$ liters from a vessel of volume $V$ and replacing with water: $\text{Final Pure Liquid} = V\left(1 - \frac{x}{V}\right)^n$.
* **Permutations, Combinations & Probability:**
  * $^nP_r = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!}$; $^nC_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$; Circular permutation of $n$ distinct items $= (n-1)!$ (or $\frac{(n-1)!}{2}$ for necklaces/garlands).
  * **Bayes' Theorem:** $P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A) \cdot P(A)}{P(B)}$; Independent events: $P(A \cap B) = P(A) \cdot P(B)$; Mutually exclusive events: $P(A \cap B) = 0$.
* **Progressions & Number System:**
  * **AP:** $T_n = a + (n-1)d$, $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$; **GP:** $T_n = ar^{n-1}$, $S_\infty = \frac{a}{1-r}$ ($|r| < 1$).
  * $\sum_{k=1}^n k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$; $\sum_{k=1}^n k^2 = \frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$; $\sum_{k=1}^n k^3 = \left[\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\right]^2$.
  * $\text{Product of two numbers} = \text{HCF} \times \text{LCM}$.

### 2.2 General Intelligence & Logical Reasoning Shortcuts
* **Clocks:**
  * Angle between hour and minute hands at `H:M`: $\theta = \left|30H - \frac{11}{2}M\right|^\circ$.
  * Hands coincide **11 times in 12 hours** (**22 times in 24 hours** — every $65\frac{5}{11}$ minutes); Hands are at $90^\circ$ **22 times in 12 hours** (**44 times in 24 hours**); Hands are $180^\circ$ opposite **11 times in 12 hours** (**22 times in 24 hours**).
* **Calendars (Odd Days):**
  * Ordinary year ($365\text{ days}$) $= \mathbf{1\text{ odd day}}$; Leap year ($366\text{ days}$) $= \mathbf{2\text{ odd days}}$.
  * Century leap year must be divisible by **400** (e.g., 1600, 2000 are leap years; 1700, 1800, 1900 are **not**).
  * Odd days in centuries: **$100\text{ years} = 5$**, **$200\text{ years} = 3$**, **$300\text{ years} = 1$**, **$400\text{ years} = 0$**.
* **Syllogisms (Venn Diagrams & Complementary Pairs for "Either-Or"):**
  * An **"Either-Or"** case requires: (1) Both conclusions are individually false/uncertain, (2) Same subject and predicate, and (3) Forms a complementary pair: **`Some + No`** or **`All + Some Not`** (Note: `All + No` is **NOT** a complementary pair!).
* **Alphabet Position Trick (`EJOTY` & Opposite Letters):**
  * **`E = 5, J = 10, O = 15, T = 20, Y = 25`**.
  * Opposite letter pairs sum to **27** (`A(1)+Z(26)=27`, `B(2)+Y(25)=27`, `C(3)+X(24)=27`).

### 2.3 High-Yield English Grammar Rules for Error Spotting & Fill-in-the-Blanks
1. **Subject-Verb Agreement:**
   * Subjects joined by **`as well as`, `along with`, `together with`, `in addition to`, `accompanied by`, `besides`** take a verb agreeing with the **FIRST subject** (e.g., *"The manager, along with his developers, **is** attending the meeting"*).
   * Subjects joined by **`Either...or`, `Neither...nor`, `Not only...but also`** take a verb agreeing with the **NEAREST subject** (Rule of Proximity).
   * **`Each`, `Every`, `Either`, `Neither`, `One of the + Plural Noun`** always take a **Singular Verb** (e.g., *"One of the servers **is** down"*).
   * **`A number of + Plural Noun`** takes a **Plural Verb**, whereas **`The number of + Plural Noun`** takes a **Singular Verb**.
2. **Conditional Clauses (If-Clauses):**
   * **Zero Conditional (Facts):** `If + Simple Present` $\rightarrow$ `Simple Present`.
   * **First Conditional (Likely Future):** `If + Simple Present` $\rightarrow$ **`will / can / may + V1`**.
   * **Second Conditional (Hypothetical/Unreal Present):** `If + Simple Past (or 'were')` $\rightarrow$ **`would / could + V1`** (*"If I **were** you, I **would patch** the server"*).
   * **Third Conditional (Impossible Past Regret):** `If + Past Perfect (had + V3)` $\rightarrow$ **`would have / could have + V3`** (*"If they **had enabled** MFA, the breach **would not have occurred**"*).
3. **Correlative Conjunction Pairs (Fixed Pairings):**
   * **`Hardly / Scarcely ... when`** (never `than`!); **`No sooner ... than`** (followed by inversion: *"No sooner **had** the alert triggered **than** the SOC responded"*).
   * **`Lest ... should`** (*"Work carefully **lest** you **should** make an error"* — never use `not` after `lest`).
   * **`Both ... and`**; **`Between ... and`**; **`From ... to`**; **`Though / Although ... yet`** (never `but`!).
   * **Preposition Traps:** `Prefer X to Y` / `Senior to` / `Junior to` / `Superior to` / `Inferior to` / `Prior to` (always take **`to`**, never `than`); `Accused of`, `Acquitted of`, `Comprise` (= consists of; never say *"comprised of"* in active voice), `Cope with` (never *"cope up with"*).

## 4. 50 High-Probability Exam Questions (MCQ / MSQ / Fill-in-the-Blank Self-Test)
